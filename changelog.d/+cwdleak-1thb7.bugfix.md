The HUD binary is chosen by one lookup. `file -p` and `note` do not stat `PATH` while filing.
A hung `PATH` entry is skipped inside one probe budget for the whole lookup, not one budget per entry.
`--hide` and `--foreground` reuse that answer, so a later stat cannot pick a different program.
A relative `PATH` entry is resolved from the directory the process is in after the park, in `PATH` order.
A root spelling that does not answer is not stored by applying `..` to the text.
