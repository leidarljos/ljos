A persona named with --as, other than the casting seat, is recorded as its own voter.
One session may cast many of them, and the tally and consensus count each one.
The same persona twice is still a repeated ballot, and a withdrawal lets that persona cast again.
A persona whose name would be read as the casting seat needs --used to name a deed.
A persona is its own voice only when --used names a real deed, one deed backs one persona on an issue, and that voice never folds into the seat.
