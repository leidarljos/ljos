`ljos start` and `ljos_start` print a Cloud-agent brief rules block for a cloud-agent phrase, a kit beside build, land, or handoff, a format-patch or patch-only task, a `--brief` flag, or a cloud label.
The block is the stored lesson whose title or text is Cloud-agent brief rules, read through the recall a start already runs, and a lesson past 4096 bytes is cut.
The built-in rules stand in when that lesson is absent, and the handoff directory in them is `LJOS_HANDOFF_DIR` or a placeholder when that setting is unset.
An ordinary task, including one that only says brief, bundle, patch, kit, or handoff, prints nothing.
