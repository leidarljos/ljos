Seat ballots are signed: `ljos vote` signs each seat ballot with the seat's deedar host key and pins the key on the tracker on first use (`.ljos-seat-keys`). The push gate and `ljos consensus` count a seat ballot only when its newest signature covers that voter, issue and choice under the pinned key; unsigned, forged or altered ballots are named and refused. `LJOS_UNSIGNED_BALLOTS=allow` lets an older tracker through on purpose.
