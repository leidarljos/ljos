One ordinary word of a longer prompt no longer brings a memory in. It used to, whenever the cosine sat within 0.05 of the prompt's best, and unrelated prompts then received memories that share `test`, `commit` or `build`. Below the encoder's confidence line a memory is handed over when an alias names it, when two content words are shared, or when the prompt has two content words or fewer, so the overlap is at least half of what it says. The encoder's first hit at a cosine of 0.66 or more still comes in with no shared word, whether the lesson is standing or an episode.
