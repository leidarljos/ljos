A value joined to its flag with an equals sign is that value, even when the text looks like a flag.
A flag's value may start with a hyphen, including a lesson or a body.
A title or finish lesson that starts with a hyphen goes after `--`, so unknown flags stay usage errors.
Text forwarded to vissue is bound as a value, so a title, body or note beginning with a hyphen reaches the tracker unchanged.
A retry does not write a note twice when a slow vissue process exits late.
