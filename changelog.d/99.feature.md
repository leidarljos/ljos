Feature work runs one loop the seat remembers: design, implement and review each child, then a panel vote. `ljos workflow PARENT --start feature` notes `workflow: feature` on the parent, tags it `workflow-feature`, and files a `design:` child; run again, it files nothing new. `ljos workflow PARENT` prints the round, the stage and the next step, read from the parent's children by their `stage-*` tag or their title (`design:`, `impl:`, `fix:`, `review:`, `panel rN`). `ljos workflow PARENT --round` files the next `panel rN` decision once every child is closed. `ljos finish PARENT --close` is refused, with nothing written, until the latest round passed and no child is open. A round passes when every panel persona has cast, no veto persona voted `revise`, and `pass` holds a share of at least 0.6 that is not a tie. The panel is tester, senior-engineer, junior-dev, ceo and cto, with the tester's veto, read through one settings function that returns these defaults. There is no round cap, and nothing in the loop waits on the person: a round that does not pass names its reasons and the fix children to file. A sitting on the parent binds the new `feature` playbook, and a sitting on a child prints the parent's status under `== workflow`. The workflow outlives every sitting: finish and release drop the playbook note, not the workflow. A title with the word "feature" still binds `sit`.
