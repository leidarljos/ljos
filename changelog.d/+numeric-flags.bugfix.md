A negative number on `--designers`, `--slack`, `--beta`, `--share`, `--gen` and the other numeric flags is read as that number.
When the range excludes it, the line names the flag and the value.
A habit reading may be negative, and the number is stored.
`--failures` follows the same rule: `-1` and `--failures=-1` name the flag and the value, and the command exits 2.
