`session_actor` and `stamped_sessions` now read `conversation_id` from the hook payload instead of consulting the process environment, and refuse when `conversation_id` or another runner's session key is present with an unusable value. For a `sand-sub` id, the parent's seat records and process match only through an ancestor whose own environment carries a usable `CURSOR_CONVERSATION_ID`, and `subagent_brief` tells child tasks to open a sitting of their own when needed. Unbound calls read the conversation id from the outermost shell or caller instead of an intermediate process, `live_sitting` compares the conversation tag on the tracker, and `ljos_sitting` over MCP covers the conversation's writes both with and without `_meta`.
