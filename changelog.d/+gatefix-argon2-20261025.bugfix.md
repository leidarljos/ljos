`session_actor` and `stamped_sessions` now read `conversation_id` from the hook payload instead of consulting the process environment, and refuse when `conversation_id` is present with an unusable value. For a `sand-sub` id, the parent's seat records and process match only through an ancestor whose own environment carries a usable `CURSOR_CONVERSATION_ID`, and `subagent_brief` tells subagents to open a sitting of their own when needed.
