# overnight

Unattended sitting. Same recipe until finish or release. Mid-turn re-reads this file. A new task is a new sitting.

## 1. Sit

`ljos sitting ISSUE --playbook overnight`. Seed the five recipes if the pack does not hold them.

## 2. One step

Do the next step of the bound playbook. Do not pick up a previous delegate across phases.

## 3. Stop clean

Finish or release. Leave the ticket in a state the next sitting can claim. Do not leave a panel half-seated.
